Restore a subscription

On the plan screen, tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Cancel a subscription

Open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Siwa. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Your data is stored only on your iPhone, so this erases it there.

Measurement tips

  • Use even, bright light facing you.
  • Hold the phone upright at eye level and follow the on-screen guides.
  • Keep a relaxed face with no smile.
