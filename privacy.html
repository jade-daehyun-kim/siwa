Privacy Policy

Siwa · Effective November 18, 2026

Siwa is made by an individual developer. This policy explains what information the Siwa app uses and where it is kept. In short: your face photos and results stay on your iPhone. We do not run servers that receive your data.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • Camera and face measurements. Siwa uses the front camera to measure the balance of your face. Camera images are processed on your iPhone. Photos you save, measurement results, and your care plan are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Face data is not used to identify you. Siwa measures distances between points on your face to suggest exercises. It does not recognize who you are, and it does not use this data for advertising.
  • Your account. When you use Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an account identifier and, the first time only, the name and email you choose to share (Apple may give a private relay email instead). Siwa keeps these only in your iPhone's secure storage (Keychain).

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To match a subscription to your account, the app attaches a random identifier to purchases, which Apple receives with the purchase.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.
  • No collection of your data on our servers.

Deleting your data

  • In the app, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This erases your photos, results, plan, and account record from your iPhone.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.
  • To stop using Sign in with Apple with Siwa, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Sign in with Apple.

Children

Siwa is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age required where you live.

Not medical advice

Siwa offers general wellness exercises. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
